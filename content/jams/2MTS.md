## Overview
20 minutes till showtime was my contribution to the GMTK2026 Game Jam that was held over 22-26th of July and lasted 96 hours.

The Jam had the theme of "countdown", with this theme we decided to go for a noir detective, murder mystery style game. The initial idea was for the murders to be caused by a gun, and the casings of the bullets used would have numbers on them, counting down towards the last murder. Unfortunately we had to pivot to a new plot with only 6 hours left of the jam, due to conflicts with team members schedules, and the initial plot being way too complex for what we actually had time to create.

My role for the jam, was to create all the art assets we needed. I achieved this with the use of Aseprite. this was my first time using the software, so had to quickly learn how to navigate it correctly, as well as it being my first time creating Isometric pixel art. this was also my second time using Godot game engine, so still relatively new to using it.

Our programmer, [Abby](https://just-a-bee.github.io/) was a great help in helping me with any problems I came across when using Godot during the jam.
